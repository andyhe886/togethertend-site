How it works

One shared view for the whole family

TogetherTend organizes and reminds, and shows the whole family when something needs attention.

A shared feed

Everyone sees the same log, like "Margaret marked her morning pill taken."

Appointments

Reminders go to the family, and you can see who is driving.

Medication tracking

Doses marked taken or skipped, missed doses flagged, and a heads-up before a refill runs out.

Everyone in the loop

Brothers, sisters and Mom all see what she has chosen to share, in one place.

Her care, her choice

Mom stays in charge

TogetherTend starts with Mom's permission. Nobody sees anything until she says yes.

  • Family members see nothing by default.
  • She chooses, one area at a time: medications, appointments or notes.
  • She can change her mind whenever she likes.

In the pilot

A simple pill screen for parents

Big buttons. Drawn pills. A smiling sun for morning and a sleepy moon for evening. Mom taps one button when she has taken her pill, or taps “Taken” right on the reminder without opening the app.

In her language: 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español or Français, with a button that reads her pills aloud.

Made for parents who do not read English well, which is true for many immigrant families. Pictures lead, words follow.
